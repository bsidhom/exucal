## Findings

The display rule holds as the pages state it. One function, `show` (`src/core/display.ts:238-284`), decides a line's kind in the order the page gives; `=` is the radius's to decide and every digit held must be shown (`src/core/display.ts:263`); certification is the two ends rounding alike (`src/core/ball.ts:2069-2088`), scanned from the digits asked down (`src/core/ball.ts:2170-2189`); in exact mode the rounding mode acts at the display and in `round` alone (`src/core/builtins/round.ts:309`); the outcome writes the relation, the notes and the lines (`src/outcome.ts:347-377`, `504-517`), and the component draws the uncertified dot and names it for assistive technology (`src/unit-converter.ts:105-117`, `296-305`). The claims hold against the code with two corrections, both at the display's edges:

- **The *partial* kind is reachable today**, and not only through the undesigned two-argument `approx`: an identity through an irrational step whose value sits on a rounding boundary (`sqrt(2)^2 * 1.000000025`, seven of eight), and any ball straddling a grid point under a directed mode (`approx(1)`, `cosh(1e-999999999)` under `floor`, none of eight). The retry loop keeps it from the display except at the ceiling (`src/core/pipeline.ts:334-374`). The condition for "none certified" in the page and in the `Shown` type's comment (`src/core/display.ts:190-192`) is "a rounding tie at the digits asked", which describes neither route.
- **The one literal that does not parse back is not written**: `9.5e1000000000000000000` at one digit throws at `src/outcome.ts:396`, since `numberExpr` rebuilds every literal from the display's text through `parseDecimal`, which refuses a value past the bound (`src/core/scaled.ts:187-197`). The component would surrender its render on that input. The bound of a zero kind carried past the edge has no such problem: it goes into the note as text (`src/outcome.ts:509`), and `(approx(1) - approx(1)) * 9.5e1000000000000000000 * 5e4048` shows `within 1e+1000000000000000001 of zero` as the page says.

The notes' exact wordings are the outcome's, in `noteOf` (`src/outcome.ts:504-517`): "N of M digits are certain", "within B of zero", "the digits are not certified", "a step could not be certified at the precision limit". They are not in `src/messages.ts`, which the plan's §1.1.6 item 1 names; the warnings of an assumed condition are (`describeAssumption`, `src/outcome.ts:232`). The *unbounded* kind and its note cannot appear today: `UNBOUNDED` enters a result only from an unbounded operand (`src/core/ball.ts:652,826,833,1261,1456`; `src/core/builtin.ts:494-498`), and nothing originates one.

The ratio notation's five rules hold, in the plan's order and the page's: a whole number entire at the width, by the decimal display at the width (`src/core/display.ts:252-255`); the fraction with the power of ten multiplied back when its parts fit together (`131-148`); the canonical significand times its power of ten when that fits and the denominator is not one (`149-154`); else the decimal display at the display digits (`263-283`); an inexact or uncertified value never reaches it (`243-247`). The width counts significant digits for a whole number, the two parts together for a ratio, and never the sign, the slash or the power of ten; it ranges over `[1, 1000]` depending on nothing (`src/settings.ts:477`); the notation reaches `show` in exact mode alone (`src/outcome.ts:202-215`), and the rounding mode plays no part in a ratio (`src/core/display.test.ts:503-529`). Where a ratio is written: parenthesized before a unit and bare alone, as the pages say, and bare before the reciprocal's `×` (`in = 1/36 × 3 ft`, `src/outcome.test.ts:97`), where `devdocs/notation.md:147` and `docs/settings.md:77` say parenthesized and give a reason the printer does not implement; the printer parenthesizes by the grammar's levels alone (`src/core/print.ts:136-147`), and the outcome composes the tree (`src/outcome.ts:411-443`). The history is squashed, so git does not say which came first.

The copy path is as the owner decided (§12, question 11): the clipboard gets the printer's text of the composed expression with `*` (`src/outcome.ts:167-169`), never the screen's, and `×` is not in the grammar. `format` is `%g`-like as N12 says, and nothing goes through a `number`. The outcome does not parse the `want` again: the pipeline's `Conversion` carries `haveExpr` and `wantExpr` (`src/core/pipeline.ts:215-262`), and the outcome parses only the `have` of a definition line (`src/outcome.ts:380-384`); `devdocs/architecture.md` says otherwise twice, and so does the plan's §1.2.4.

The docs tail paragraphs (`docs/settings.md:192-205`), D8's context, state the model correctly: every digit asked for or a note, `=` for an exact value every digit of which is shown, `≈` for rounding or an inexact step, `2^(1/2) m` in `2^(1/2) m` is `≈ 1` (live: `≈ 1 × 2^(1/2) m`), a value that cannot be told from zero cannot be divided by. The `> Rounding` bullet's examples hold live (`0.125` to `0.12`, `0.135` to `0.14`, `39.3700787401` rounded down against `39.3700787402`).

The code change §12, question 8 assigns to this unit was not made in this run, per the skill; it is proposed below with a test, since nothing in the tree asserts the partial note's text today (`src/core/display.test.ts` checks the kind, `src/outcome.test.ts` the zero and the uncertified notes).
