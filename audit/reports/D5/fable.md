## Findings

### The family-level rules against the code (`#The rules every built-in follows`, `#Huge and tiny arguments`)

Every bullet was checked; the family-level rules hold, with the one stale bullet tagged above and the following precisions.

- **The kernel's ball, rounded in no mode; settled by the evaluator; one step in decimal mode.** Confirmed. Every module hands back `fixedToBall(…, workingDigits(context))` or an exact ball (`src/core/builtins/exp.ts:87-91`, `log.ts:80`, `trig.ts:72`, `inverse-trig.ts:161`, `hyperbolic.ts:194-198`), whose contract rounds the midpoint onto the digits and adds the rounding's bound, "nothing rounded in a mode" (`src/core/fixed.ts:241-248`). The call is one step through `stepQuantity`, which settles in exact mode and certifies and rounds in decimal mode (`src/core/evaluate.ts:834-850`; `src/core/quantity.ts:79-88`); `approx` alone declares `decimal: "identity"` (`src/core/builtins/approx.ts:41`; `src/core/builtin.ts:175-182`). That no built-in's `apply` ever receives a decimal-mode context rests on `stepQuantity`'s mechanics in `quantity.ts`, which is A4's module; the contract read here says the step, not the built-in, certifies and rounds.
- **An exact case returns an exact ball; the rest is `≈` however round it looks.** Confirmed: `ln(8) / ln(2) ≈ 3` and `log(4, 8) ≈ 1.5` in the harness; the exact cases are `ok(exact(…))` throughout (`log.ts:78,127-133,165`; `pow.ts:90-91,104`; `trig.ts:68,82,96`; `inverse-trig.ts:149,173,197`; `hyperbolic.ts:154,168,182,208,245,289`; `roots.ts` through `rootBall`, `src/core/ball.ts:1253-1257`).
- **An argument of unbounded radius.** Confirmed: `unboundedOf` (`builtin.ts:494-498`) is the first branch of every entry point, the midpoint held to the domain by the recursive call.
- **Two parts, the module and the kernel routine.** The split is as described; the kernel's propagation and the `sin(1 ± 1e-20)` radius are algorithm claims and were not verified (not this unit's).
- **A tiny argument keeps its digits.** Confirmed: `extraBits` (`builtin.ts:387-394`, held to the significand limit) is applied by `sinCos` (`trig.ts:127`), `atanKernel` (`inverse-trig.ts:441`), `tanh` (`hyperbolic.ts:195`), `asinh` (`hyperbolic.ts:225-226`) and the hyperbolic inverses' logarithm (`hyperbolic.ts:420-428`); the harness gives `sin(1e-50 radian) ≈ 1e-50`, `atan(1e-50) ≈ 1e-50 radian`, `asinh(1e-50)`, `atanh(1e-50)` and `acosh(1 + 1e-30) ≈ 1.4142136e-15`.
- **A wide argument gets a trivial bound.** Confirmed for every function named: about a fifth of a radian and zero within one for the sine and cosine (`src/core/fixed-trig.ts:44-46,110-111`), one radian and zero within two for the arctangent (`fixed-trig.ts:60-61`), one half for `asinh` with the radius added at the midpoint (`hyperbolic.ts:218-224`), an error of one half for `tanh`, zero within one (`hyperbolic.ts:190-192`), and for `exp` the exponential of the signed upper end formed exactly (`src/core/fixed-exp.ts:31-39`), the largest magnitude for the hyperbolic pair (`fixed-exp.ts:211-212`). `hullOfEnds` and `moveBetween` are as described (`builtin.ts:533-589`); their callers are D7's.
- **Domain errors and `unresolved` at an edge; touching counts as straddling.** Confirmed. `position` returns `touchingAbove` or `touchingBelow` for a ball with one end on the point (`src/core/ball.ts:355-379`); `checkPositive` counts `touchingBelow` and `at` as not positive and everything else but `above` as `unresolved` at zero (`builtin.ts:341-355`); `complementRoot` reads the position against `±1` before squaring and makes a touch `unresolved` (`inverse-trig.ts:354-370`); `acosh` and `atanh` do the same at `1` and `-1` (`hyperbolic.ts:249-256,290-309`); the exponent of a zero base goes through `sideOfZero`, where a touch straddles (`pow.ts:101-111`; `builtin.ts:363-376`). The harness: `asin(approx(1))`, `acosh(approx(1))`, `atanh(0.5 + approx(0.5))` and `log(approx(1), 5)` all read "cannot be told from 1 at this precision"; `ln(0)` and `log(2, 0)` are the domain error; `pow(0, 1 - approx(1))` is "cannot be told from zero". The edges a message names are exactly the five listed (`src/messages.ts:413-419`, the rounding boundary written to twelve digits).
- **Refused at the exponent bound before anything is computed.** Confirmed for this unit's functions: `classifyExp` (`ball.ts:1692-1705`, `underflow` on the side of a negative argument), `classifyHyperbolic` (`ball.ts:1714-1725`, "against the top edge alone", so `sinh` and `cosh` of either sign are too large: the harness refuses `sinh(-1e30)` and `cosh(-1e30)` as too large and gives `sinh(1e6) ≈ 1.5166077e+434294`), `classifyPower` through `powLogSpace` (`ball.ts:1476-1492,1765-1792`); a ball across an edge is `unresolved` at `bound` and retried (`exp.ts:81-82`; the harness gives `exp(approx(1e999) - approx(1e999 - 5)) ≈ 148.41316`). `tanh` has no guard (`hyperbolic.ts:176-199`; the harness gives `tanh(1e999999999) ≈ 1`); the figure "about 181,727" is a derived constant stated identically in `hyperbolic.ts:36-39` and was not recomputed here.
