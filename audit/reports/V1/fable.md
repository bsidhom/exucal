## Findings

### 1. How the page holds up

The page is accurate in its mechanism almost everywhere. The A units read all but two of its sections against the code and corrected single sentences, never a design; this run read the four sections the plan names as not covered by an A unit, `#The kernel's boundary` and `#Roots and powers`, which none had read, and `#Memoized constants` and `#Failures`, which A3 and A5 had read for their claims alone, sentence by sentence against their modules, and ran every example they give (the table in 5). What differs from the code, beyond the A units' rows:

- **The log-space exit's memos are served, not recomputed.** `#The kernel's boundary` says the conversion of a `Floating` in log space computes "the two memos … for the call at the ceiling's bits plus the exponent's". `floatingToBall` asks for `ln 2` and `ln 10` at the working bits plus the exponent's bit length plus a guard of 24 (`src/core/digits.ts:94-104`), `lnFixed` asks for `ln 2` 33 bits above that (`src/core/fixed-log.ts:85-86`), and the memo holds the ceiling's bits plus 256 (`src/core/constants.ts:180`, `:196-207`): an exponent within the bound has at most 62 bits, so the largest request at the ceiling pass lies within the margin, and the margin paragraph of `#Memoized constants` describes exactly this. Run: after `exp(1e6) - exp(1e6)` ran all nine passes, the memos of `ln 2` and `ln 10` hold 13,709 bits, the ceiling's 13,453 plus the margin. The sentence predates the margin, which the constants' header says was raised from 64 because the old margin "had them recomputed for every such call"; it goes with its timing.
- **The square root's interval is the verified bound, not the integer roots of the ends.** `sqrtFixed` is Newton on the reciprocal square root, multiplications only, and its error is `D s / (2 s^2 - D)` verified by squaring (`src/core/fixed.ts:486-549`); the header names the integer square root of the argument's ends as the earlier version (`:28-33`). The page states the verified bound and then adds "The square root's interval is exact, its ends the integer roots of the argument's ends", which is the replaced design.
- **Two kinds of request pass the memo, not one.** The constants' header names the trigonometric reduction at a larger argument and a logarithm of `beta` at a larger one as the requests computed for the call and not kept (`src/core/constants.ts:63-65`); the page names the first alone. The `beta` case is V3's to confirm in `src/core/builtins/beta.ts`; the page's sentence should not exclude it.
- **The Bernoulli ratios are not memoized "the same way".** The constants hold one value at the ceiling plus the margin, replaced for a higher ceiling; the ratios' memo holds one run keyed by the count the series reserve and the bits they ask for, grows on either, and serves the bits asked for plus the same margin (`src/core/bernoulli-ratios.ts:17-27`). What they share is the margin and its reason. The footprint, and #103, belong to the Gamma family's page.
- **The `pow` prediction that #89 describes is gone.** `rational.ts`'s `pow` predicts `floor(|n| log2 p) + 1` with `log2 p` taken from the part's top bits (`src/core/rational.ts:410-437`), which is what the issue's "Done when" asks for, and every row the issue lists agrees today: `2^40000` is `≈ 1.5842604e+12041` as `2^20000 * 2^20000` is, `3^41000` is `≈ 9.3636141e+19561` as `3^20500 * 3^20500` is, `2^65000` is a value, `3^1000000000` stays `tooLarge` by prediction and `2^-30000` with about 69,659 bits. The page's clause "`pow`'s size prediction refuses powers the node check would admit" is stale twice over, since the node check it names is the measuring check that `#The significand limit` says no longer exists (the evaluator asserts). #88 stands: `2^1e-30000` is refused with about 99,659 bits "however it is computed" where `pow(2, 1e-30000)` is `≈ 1`, and `1^1e999999999` is rejected as typed where `pow(1, 1e999999999)` is `= 1`.
- **#79 reproduces.** `cbrt(3e24 + (approx(2e51) - approx(2e51)))` throws "Newton's root is not within half of its argument" from `verifiedRoot` (`src/core/fixed.ts:695-697`), the one place a user's value reaches a throw, as the page says in `#Roots and powers`; `#Failures` states the rule the throw breaks and need not repeat the issue.
